# Can't Hurt Me (Hindi summary): shot plan

One continuous shot through one world. The world is built around the cover: the
runner's **hill** is its spine. **His past lies in the dark valley below the hill**
(night shift, childhood, the couch). **His achievements sit in the sky and the land to
the right** (road, pull-up sky, beach, boat, trail). **Each lesson is a station further
up the hillside**, and the climb ends at the summit, then pulls back to the cover.
Guide character: the runner silhouette cut from the cover. Callbacks: the beach and the
boat seen in the intro come back on the TV, in the cookie jar and at "who will carry the boats".
