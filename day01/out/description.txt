On 6 August 1945, Tsutomu Yamaguchi turned back to fetch his personal seal, and that detour put him three kilometres from the centre of Hiroshima when the first atomic bomb exploded. Three days later, burned and bandaged, he was back at work in Nagasaki, explaining what he had seen to a supervisor who did not believe him, when the second one exploded. He is the only person officially recognised as a survivor of both.

This is the story of the seal he went back for, the train journey home, and the sixty-five years he spent mostly not talking about it.
